COLOR SYSTEM
==================================================

Use a near-black foundation rather than pure black.

Primary background:
deep near-black

Primary text:
off-white

Secondary text:
muted gray

Accent:
restrained electric cyan / blue-violet family

Do not flood the interface with accent color.

Accent should primarily identify:
- active states
- links
- important metadata
- graphs
- interactive controls
- selected states

Create semantic tokens rather than hardcoding colors everywhere.
